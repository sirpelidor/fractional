Fractional Leadership

Experienced leadership without hiring a full-time CTO/CIO.

When your business needs more senior technology attention than your current team can provide—but doesn't justify another full-time executive—I step in to provide direction, accountability, and executive-level ownership.

This may be a fit when:

  • Technology is consuming too much of your attention
  • Your team or vendors need stronger direction and accountability
  • Important initiatives lack clear ownership or priorities
  • You need leadership judgment on tech, but not a full-time CTO/CIO

How I help:

  • Set priorities around your business goals
  • Guide major investments and decisions
  • Align leadership, internal teams, vendors, and specialists
  • Surface risks and tradeoffs before they become expensive problems
  • Take ownership of moving important initiatives forward

The result:

Your business gets the leadership it needs while you get the visibility and confidence to focus on running it.

Project & Delivery Turnaround

Get an important initiative moving again

When a critical project is slipping, stalled, or losing leadership confidence, I help determine what's actually preventing progress and establish a practical path forward.

This may be a fit when:

  • Deadlines keep slipping despite everyone's effort
  • Costs are increasing without enough visible progress
  • Teams, vendors, or stakeholders disagree about what needs to happen next
  • Leadership no longer has confidence in the plan or delivery

How I help:

  • Separate symptoms from the constraints actually blocking progress
  • Clarify priorities, ownership, dependencies, and unresolved decisions
  • Establish measurable outcomes and checkpoints
  • Align the people needed to move the initiative forward
  • Give leadership clear visibility without pulling you into the weeds

The result:

Regain control, restore momentum, and create a clear path toward delivery.

Investment & Decision Advisory

Make important decisions with confidence before committing significant money, time, or resources.

When the right direction isn't obvious—or every option involves tradeoffs—I provide an independent business-and-technology perspective to help you determine the best path forward.

This may be a fit when:

  • You're deciding whether to build, buy, modernize, replace, or stay with what you have
  • You're questioning whether a major investment is worth continuing
  • Vendors or internal teams are recommending different directions
  • You're evaluating where AI, automation, or new capabilities could create meaningful business value

How I help:

  • Start with the business outcome, not the solution
  • Identify the constraints that truly cannot be compromised
  • Separate immediate needs from capabilities that can wait
  • Evaluate realistic options across cost, risk, timing, and business impact
  • Bring in specialized expertise when the decision requires it

The result:

Understand your options and tradeoffs well enough to make the decision confidently—and know why you're making it.
