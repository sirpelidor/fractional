How I Work

From a pressing concern to a clear way forward.


1. Put the real issue on the table

We discuss what's bothering you. I ask direct questions and challenge assumptions.

Together, we map the situation on a whiteboard or paper—making the problem easier to see and tackle.


2. Check the picture and recommend a direction

I share relevant experience. If we need more evidence, I propose a diagnostic review with your team, vendors, or systems. We agree on the review's scope, fee, and timing upfront.

Once the review is complete, we compare the findings with our original picture and outline priorities, a roadmap, and measures of success. You decide whether to proceed.


3. Choose the support you need

  • I lead: I take responsibility for moving the agreed work forward with your team and vendors.
  • I advise: Your team leads. I help with decisions, tradeoffs, and progress reviews.
  • I bring in specialists: I help find the right expertise and define my role alongside it.

We agree on responsibilities and fees for any further engagement before it starts. You can also proceed independently.
